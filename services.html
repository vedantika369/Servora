FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Servora, vehicle servicing, bookings, inspections, pricing, and service updates.

How can I book a vehicle service?

Create a Servora account or log in, register your vehicle, select the vehicle you want to service, choose the required service, describe the problem, select your preferred date, and submit the booking.

Do I need to register my vehicle before booking?

Yes. Your vehicle needs to be registered in your Servora account before you can book a service. This allows us to keep your vehicle details and service history organized.

Are the prices shown on Servora final?

Service prices shown on Servora are starting or estimated prices. The final cost depends on the actual condition of your vehicle and the work required after inspection.

What happens if another problem is found?

Our mechanic will inspect the vehicle and identify any additional problems. If extra repairs or parts are required, the additional work and estimated cost can be communicated before proceeding.

Can I track my service status?

Yes. You can check your booking and service progress from your Servora customer dashboard. Status information can be updated as your vehicle moves through the service process.

Can I view my previous service records?

Yes. Completed services are stored in your Service History so you can review previous services, vehicle details, mechanic notes, and payment information.

Can I get an invoice for my completed service?

Yes. Once a service is completed, the invoice section can display the service details, final amount, payment status, and other relevant invoice information.

Do you provide emergency roadside assistance?

Servora can provide information about emergency roadside assistance for situations such as breakdowns, flat tyres, battery problems, and towing requirements. Availability and applicable charges may depend on the situation and location.

What types of vehicles can be serviced?

Servora supports vehicle service requirements for cars and two-wheelers. The available service and applicable pricing may vary depending on the vehicle type and specific service required.

How will I know when my vehicle service is completed?

Your booking status can be updated through the Servora system. Once the service is completed, the completed service can appear in your Service History and the corresponding invoice can become available.
